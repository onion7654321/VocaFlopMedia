VocaFlop uses third-party software. This reference list is not a complete license inventory of every dependency included in a packaged binary.

| --- | --- |
| Flet | https://github.com/flet-dev/flet |
| Flet Video | https://github.com/flet-dev/flet-video |

| Deno | https://github.com/denoland/deno |
| PyInstaller | https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html |

External media executables are installed separately from the application packages. Their licensing depends on the exact upstream distribution and build configuration. Before distributing a package, review the licenses and notices of the exact bundled dependencies and retain all required notices and accompanying materials. This page does not replace those obligations.
